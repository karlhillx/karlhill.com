# Hospitality & Retreat Partnerships

**More Than a Guest Experience Provider — An Experienced On-Site Partner**

I'm interested in opportunities with retreat centers, boutique hospitality properties, B&Bs, wellness destinations, eco-lodges, private estates, and intentional communities where I can bring together the many different areas of experience I've developed over a lifetime.

What I bring is an unusual combination of hospitality, property stewardship, guest experience, business and relationship management, live entertainment, wellness programming, nature-based experiences, photography and videography, and hands-on operations — along with the experience of actually living on and helping care for a busy destination property.

I'm at my best when I can become part of the fabric of a place: knowing the property, knowing the guests, working alongside the team, noticing what needs attention, creating memorable experiences, and being a trusted presence when an owner or manager can't be there.

## My Experience at Trois Estate

For two years, I lived and worked on-site at Trois Estate, a distinctive 28-room destination property in the Texas Hill Country near Enchanted Rock.

Trois was much more than a place where I worked or performed. It was my home, and over time I became deeply involved in both the guest experience and the day-to-day care, improvement, and operation of the property.

While we had a weekday office manager during regular business hours, I frequently became the primary on-site resource during evenings and weekends. On busy weekends, the property could be full with 50–60 overnight guests.

The owner also spent significant periods away from the property caring for her mother, and during those times I became her eyes, ears, and hands on the estate — someone she could trust to know what was happening, respond when something needed attention, and help keep both the guests and property cared for.

It was a responsibility I took seriously, but also one I genuinely enjoyed.

## Guest Experience & Hospitality

My involvement with guests often began several days before they arrived. I communicated directly with upcoming guests with the information they needed for their stay, welcomed and checked them in upon arrival, introduced them to the property, answered questions, and helped them settle into the experience.

Throughout their stay, I was often their go-to person for whatever arose — particularly during evenings and weekends, including:

- Pre-arrival guest communication
- Guest check-in and orientation
- Property tours
- Responding to room and property issues
- After-hours and weekend guest assistance
- Helping coordinate weekend dinner orders
- Providing local recommendations and information
- Helping guests discover experiences both on and beyond the property
- Creating a warm, personal connection throughout their stay

I have always believed that great hospitality goes beyond solving problems. It is about helping people feel welcomed, seen, heard, comfortable, and cared for.

## Guest Connection & Memorable Experiences

Much of the work I did behind the scenes at Trois was invisible to our guests — and that is often how good hospitality works. Guests did not necessarily know that I had been coordinating with housekeeping, dealing with a maintenance issue, caring for the pools, working with a contractor, picking up supplies, or helping prepare the property for their arrival.

What they did experience was the result: a welcoming property and opportunities to connect through music, sound, nature, and personal interaction.

I was fortunate to be mentioned by name in many outstanding guest reviews. What means the most to me is not simply that guests enjoyed a performance or sound bath, but that they frequently described those experiences — and the personal connections around them — as memorable parts of their stay at Trois, sometimes even as reasons they wanted to return.

**Through the eyes of the guests:** *He welcomed us. He hosted us. He was everywhere. He wore many hats. He made us feel at home. He created experiences that made us want to return.*

Those aren't simply qualities I hope to bring to a property. They are the experiences guests themselves described during my time at Trois.

One longer review especially captures the connection between an individual experience and the overall stay:

> "Keith's sound bath was something else entirely. Calming, emotional, and deeply grounding... it was the perfect experience to close out our trip. We left Trois feeling relaxed, inspired, and already planning our next visit."
> — Tatum Schmitt

## Property Stewardship & Operations

Much of what keeps a hospitality property running happens quietly behind the scenes.

I walked the estate daily, paying attention to what needed care, repair, cleaning, replenishment, improvement, or follow-up. If I couldn't handle something myself, I helped make sure the right person knew about it and followed through when needed.

I also worked closely and consistently with our housekeeping team, communicating items that needed attention and looking together for ways to continually improve the guest experience. Although we didn't share the same first language, we found ways to communicate and work effectively together.

Just as importantly, I made an effort to ensure that the people doing the work behind the scenes knew how much they were appreciated. That was part of a larger philosophy I brought to the property: I wanted our staff as well as our guests to feel seen, heard, valued, and appreciated.

- Daily property walks and general oversight
- Identifying maintenance, housekeeping, and guest-room issues
- Working closely with housekeeping to address needs and improve standards
- Coordinating with property contractors and service providers
- Serving as an on-site point of contact when the owner was away
- Caring for the indoor and outdoor swimming pools
- Grocery and supply runs for the kitchen when needed
- Responding to unexpected property and guest needs
- Helping prepare for busy weekends, events, retreats, and groups

It was very much a "see what needs doing and help get it done" environment, which suits me well. I don't need every responsibility to fall neatly inside a job description. If something affects the property, the team, or the guest experience and I can help, I generally do.

## Property & Animal Care

My sense of stewardship extends to the animals that may also call a property home. Each year, my sister entrusts me with the care of her farm for approximately two weeks, including her horse, dogs, cats, and the overall property while she is away.

Trois Estate was also home to a large population of more than 40 feral cats that became part of the character of the property and were frequently mentioned by guests. Over time, I developed a special connection with several of them — earning the unofficial reputation of being something of a "cat whisperer." A number of cats that generally kept their distance from people would come running when they saw me, and I helped look after them, spent time with them, and remained attentive to their safety, particularly when coyotes were moving through or stalking the property.

I've always had an easy and natural connection with animals, and I'm very comfortable with the additional responsibility that comes with a property where animals are part of the environment.

## Experiences, Retreats, Entertainment & Wellness

One of the things that made my role at Trois unusual was that my contribution didn't stop with hospitality and operations. I also helped create and facilitate the experiences guests came away remembering.

In addition to offering my own wellness experiences, I co-facilitated spiritual and wellness retreats with the owner and visiting outside facilitators, supporting both the programming and the overall retreat experience.

Because I lived on the property and understood its day-to-day operation, I could help bridge the worlds of retreat facilitation, hospitality, and on-site guest care — responding to what participants, facilitators, or the property needed throughout the experience.

Every weekend, I performed live music on stage in the estate's saloon, creating an intimate evening experience for overnight guests. I also facilitated sound baths and wellness experiences, incorporating acoustic guitar, Native flute, percussion, chimes, voice, and other instruments. With Enchanted Rock nearby, I led guests on guided hiking and nature experiences, bringing together my love of nature, people, storytelling, and the outdoors.

Whether welcoming someone at check-in, working alongside a visiting retreat facilitator, leading guests into nature, facilitating a sound experience, or performing music for a full house on Saturday night, my intention was much the same: help people feel welcomed, included, seen, and cared for.

## Weddings, Retreats, Events & Film Production

Trois Estate also hosted weddings, retreats, gatherings, and special events, and I frequently helped wherever needed — from guest interaction and property preparation to entertainment and general on-site support.

In 2025, Trois became the location for an approximately two-week feature-film shoot, and I served as the property's location manager during the production. That meant helping bridge the needs of a working film production with the realities of an operating hospitality property — another experience that reinforced my ability to adapt, communicate, problem-solve, and remain useful in an environment with many moving pieces.

## Photography, Video & Social Media

I also brought my photography and videography experience into my work at Trois. I regularly captured original photos and video of the property, its surroundings, events, weddings, performances, and guest experiences, and worked with the property's social media efforts to provide original visual content.

It gave me another way to contribute to the success of the estate — not only by helping create experiences, but by helping tell the story of the property to the people who might become its next guests. Photography and video continue to be an important part of how I document and share the experiences and places I'm fortunate enough to be part of.

## Currently in Sedona

Today, I continue bringing many of these same passions into my work with The Joy, a retreat and hospitality property along Oak Creek in Sedona, Arizona.

I currently lead guests on chakra-focused hikes up Joy Mountain and provide live creekside music for guests, gatherings, and special events. These experiences allow me to continue combining hospitality, music, nature, wellness, and personal connection in a destination setting — many of the same elements I found so meaningful during my years at Trois Estate.

My time at The Joy has further reinforced how much I value being part of a property where the environment itself, the experiences offered, and the way guests are personally welcomed can all contribute to something memorable.

My current work in Sedona continues to center around people, place, nature, and experience — from retreat-property programming and guided outdoor experiences to live music.

## Where My Experience Comes Together

What I valued most about my time at Trois Estate was having a direct and personal impact on both the guest experience and the success of the property itself.

I genuinely enjoyed being the person who knew what was happening across the estate — welcoming guests, solving problems, working with staff and contractors, creating experiences, performing music, supporting retreats, and simply noticing the things that could make someone's stay or the property a little better.

## Business & Relationship Management

Before entering destination hospitality, I spent decades as a professional territory and account manager, managing business relationships and territories across the country with direct sales, revenue, and quota responsibilities.

That career taught me how to build and maintain strong business relationships, understand client needs, work independently, meet performance goals, identify opportunities for growth and improvement, solve problems, and represent a business professionally.

Just as importantly, it reinforced something that has carried into my hospitality work: business success and strong human relationships don't have to be separate things. Whether working with a longtime client or welcoming a guest for the first time, I've found that people remember how well you listen, how reliably you follow through, and how you make them feel.

## A Lifetime of Music

Alongside my professional career, music has been a constant throughout my adult life. I have been performing since my early twenties, including appearances on some of the country's iconic stages and at well-known venues, while continuing to value intimate performances where the connection with the people in the room matters just as much as the stage itself.

Music isn't simply something I offer professionally. It has been part of how I connect with people for most of my life.

## Wellness, Spirituality & A Life of Exploration

My interests in wellness, spirituality, meditation, and personal growth have also been lifelong pursuits. Over the decades, they have taken me to retreat centers, yoga and meditation experiences, sacred places, and spiritual communities across the world.

Being immersed in these different environments gave me not only meaningful personal experiences, but a firsthand appreciation for what makes a retreat or contemplative space feel welcoming, intentional, restorative, and special.

Over time, these passions grew into experiences I could share with others through sound baths, meditation, Yoga Nidra, music, nature experiences, and retreat facilitation.

## A Deep Connection With Nature

Those decades of travel and exploration have also fostered a deep appreciation for nature and nature-based experiences.

Whether hiking through extraordinary landscapes, sitting beside water, exploring unfamiliar places, or simply creating opportunities for people to slow down and reconnect with the natural world, I have come to see nature itself as an important part of the guest and retreat experience.

That appreciation has naturally found its way into the experiences I now create for others.

## What I Bring to a Property

My experience doesn't fit neatly into one traditional hospitality position — and I see that as one of my greatest strengths. Depending upon the needs of a property, I can contribute through:

- **Hospitality & Guest Experience** — Welcoming guests, communication, check-ins, local knowledge, problem-solving, and creating genuine personal connection.
- **On-Site Property Stewardship** — Being a dependable resident presence who notices what needs attention, works well with staff and vendors, and helps keep things moving.
- **Business & Relationship Experience** — Decades of account management, sales responsibility, relationship building, independent decision-making, and accountability for results.
- **Live Music & Entertainment** — Acoustic performances, intimate concerts, fireside music, and special-event programming.
- **Wellness & Retreat Experiences** — Sound baths, meditation, Yoga Nidra, retreat support, spiritual programming, and restorative experiences.
- **Guided Outdoor Experiences** — Hiking, nature immersion, and meaningful ways of helping guests experience the surrounding environment.
- **Photography, Video & Content Creation** — Creating authentic visual content that captures the property, its people, and its experiences.
- **Events & Special Projects** — Supporting weddings, retreats, productions, groups, and the unexpected needs that naturally come with destination hospitality.

## What I'm Ultimately Looking For

At Trois, for perhaps the first time, all of these worlds came together in one place.

I could bring my decades of business and relationship-management experience to the success of the estate, my people skills and hospitality instincts to its guests, my music and entertainment background to its programming, my wellness and spiritual experience to retreats and restorative offerings, my love of nature to outdoor experiences, and my photography and videography skills to helping tell the property's story.

I loved having all of those parts of my life come together in service to one place and the people who visited it. And ultimately, that's what I'm hoping to continue building.

I'm interested in continuing to grow into this kind of role with the right property and community — not simply as an employee who arrives for a shift, but as a trusted presence who knows the property, knows the people, understands its purpose, notices what needs attention, and contributes wherever my experience can add value.

That might be a retreat center in the mountains, a wellness property near the ocean, a boutique B&B, an eco-lodge, a private destination estate, or a completely different environment I haven't yet imagined.

The location matters less to me than the people, the purpose, and the opportunity to contribute. For the right opportunity, I'm open to possibilities both in the United States and internationally.

I'm not simply looking for a place to perform music or facilitate sound baths. And I'm not looking exclusively for a traditional property-management position.

I'm looking for the intersection of all of it: a place where I can care for the property, care for the people, create meaningful experiences, and contribute the full range of what I've learned over a lifetime.

If you operate a retreat center, B&B, wellness destination, boutique property, private estate, or another special place and believe that kind of partnership could be valuable, I'd love to have a conversation.
