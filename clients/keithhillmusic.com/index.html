The sound

Silver and grit

Keith Hill channels the edge of rock and the soul of folk with a voice that's pure silver and grit. Originals and covers spanning decades — delivered raw, real, and full of fire.

Keith Hill plays with silver and grit — a sound forged in classic rock, steeped in country soul, and dusted with the storytelling spirit of folk. As a seasoned performer, his voice carries the echo of long backroads and the fire of city lights while his guitar work blends raw edge with melodic finesse. Delivering both heartfelt originals and high-energy covers that span the decades, Keith's voice feels both lived-in and timeless.

Raised on vinyl and shaped by generations of sound, Keith channels the spirit of classic songwriters and legendary rockers, crafting a musical experience that feels both nostalgic and fresh.

For venues & bookers

Keith Hill brings a raw, soulful blend of rock and folk to every stage, with a voice that's equal parts silver and grit. His setlist spans the ’70s through the 2000s, weaving standout originals with reimagined covers from iconic rockers and legendary songwriters. Known for his charming presence and authentic delivery, Keith offers a dynamic live experience that resonates with fans of classic sounds and fresh storytelling alike. With a voice that commands attention and a presence that invites connection, Keith is built for stages that value craft, conviction, and crowd connection.
