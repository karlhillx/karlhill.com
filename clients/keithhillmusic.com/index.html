The thread

Different worlds. Same intention.

At first glance, performing live music, guiding Jeep tours through Sedona’s red rocks, facilitating sound baths, and helping care for a destination property might seem like very different worlds.

To me, they’ve always been connected.

I’ve spent much of my life creating experiences with and for other people — sometimes from a stage, sometimes in the stillness of a sound bath, sometimes bouncing down a red-rock trail in a Jeep, and sometimes simply being the person who makes sure someone feels welcomed and cared for when they arrive at a special place.

Music has been part of my life since my early twenties. Over the decades, I’ve had the opportunity to perform on some of the country’s iconic stages and at memorable venues, while never losing my appreciation for the intimacy of playing for a small group of people where genuine connection matters more than the size of the room.

Alongside music, I spent decades in professional territory and account management, traveling, building and managing business relationships, and carrying direct sales and quota responsibilities. That part of my life taught me how to listen, communicate, work independently, solve problems, follow through, and understand that successful relationships — whether in business or hospitality — are built on trust.

Running alongside both of those worlds has been a lifelong interest in travel, nature, spirituality, wellness, meditation, yoga, retreats, photography, and exploration. Those interests have taken me to remarkable places and retreat experiences around the world and gradually became experiences I could share with others through sound, music, meditation, nature, and personal connection.

For two years, those different parts of my life came together while I lived and worked at Trois Estate, a 28-room destination property in the Texas Hill Country. I performed live music every weekend, facilitated sound baths, guided hiking experiences, supported retreats, weddings and special events, created photography and video content, welcomed and cared for guests, and became a trusted on-site presence helping steward the property and its day-to-day needs.

Today, that journey continues in Sedona. I guide scenic, off-road, and vortex Jeep tours through the red rocks, create custom hiking experiences, perform live music, and bring music, nature, and wellness experiences to retreat and hospitality settings.

Over the years, I’ve come to believe that the experiences we remember most aren’t necessarily the biggest or most elaborate ones. They’re the moments when we feel connected — to a place, to another person, to nature, to music, or sometimes simply back to ourselves.

Whether I’m holding a guitar, playing a flute among the red rocks, guiding someone into a new landscape, facilitating a sound bath, or welcoming someone to a property, that’s ultimately what I’m hoping to create:

A meaningful experience. A genuine connection. And perhaps a memory someone carries home with them.
