About

In a world filled with noise, we offer stillness. In the quiet, we remember who we are. Each session is an invitation to return to yourself, to your breath, to the deep river of calm beneath the surface of thought.

Guided by Keith Hill, each sound journey is crafted with crystal singing bowls, tuned percussion gongs, flutes, harmonic tones, vocal intonations, and sacred silence, weaving a field of frequency that gently dissolves tension, clears energy blocks, and restores alignment.

Whether you are seeking clarity, emotional release, spiritual connection, or deep rest, these sound baths are a sanctuary for your soul.

The practice

What is a sound bath?

We live in rhythm — with breath, with heartbeat, with the unseen hum of the universe. But in the noise of the world, we forget how to listen.

A sound bath is an energetic cleansing, a meditative immersion in frequency and resonance. Using crystal singing bowls, tuned percussion gongs, flutes, harmonic tones, vocal intonations, and sacred silence, this session invites you into deep rest, where the body heals, the nervous system rebalances, and the spirit remembers stillness.

You do not need to do anything. Just lie back. Let the vibrations move through you. Allow what no longer serves to dissolve. This is your return to self. A remembering through sound.
