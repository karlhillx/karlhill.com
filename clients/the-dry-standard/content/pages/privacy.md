Updated 19 September 2026.

The Dry Standard is an independent editorial site. It does not sell visitor data, run advertising networks, or keep reader accounts. Questions: [drinkdrystandard@gmail.com](mailto:drinkdrystandard@gmail.com).

## What you send us

The [product submission](industry/submit/) and [partnerships](industry/partnerships/) forms collect the fields on those pages: typically a name, email, organization, product details, and a message. A hidden honeypot field is ignored except to discard spam. Direct email to the address above is treated the same way.

We use that information to reply, to consider a product for the editorial queue, and to handle business inquiries. Submissions are not published automatically, and they are not sold.

A copy is stored in a private inbox on the site host. Another copy is emailed to `drinkdrystandard@gmail.com`, which is hosted by Google under [Google’s privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The forms are rate-limited, which briefly records the requesting IP address to blunt spam.

We keep correspondence as long as it is useful for a reply or an editorial record. Write if you want a record corrected or deleted.

## Cookies

Most pages do not set a session cookie. The industry forms use a short-lived session cookie so the form can be submitted safely (CSRF protection). There is no advertising cookie and no login cookie.

## Visits

The site may record named on-page events in the browser — for example opening a review or a “where to buy” link. Those events do not include form fields or message text. No third-party analytics product is wired up.

The host may keep ordinary web logs (IP address, browser, pages requested) for security and operations. Outbound retailer and producer links leave this site; those destinations have their own policies.

## Samples

We do not publish a receiving address. If a sample is useful, shipping instructions go out by email. A parcel you send is for independent tasting, not a public listing of your contact details.
