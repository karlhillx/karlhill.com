Cider is the category most likely to hide behind "alcohol-free." Apple juice sparkling is a legitimate drink. It is not cider unless yeast did some work, or a producer took alcohol out of cider that already existed.

## Ask whether it was cider first

Dealcoholized cider starts as fermented apple (or pear) and then loses ethanol. Alternatives are juice, botanicals, and carbonation dressed in a 750 ml bottle. Eric Bordelet's sparkling pear is closer to the orchard than a grocery "NA cider" with no ferment story. Read the method line on our reviews before you buy a case.

## Acid is the structure alcohol used to be

Once the ethanol leaves, sweetness rushes in. The bottles that still taste like cider keep acid and a little tannin. If the only impression is apple soda, you bought soda.

## Chill hard, pour like cider

Serve cold, in a wine glass if the producer made a wine-sized bottle, in a pint if it was built for a pub. Warm NA cider tastes like dessert topping.
