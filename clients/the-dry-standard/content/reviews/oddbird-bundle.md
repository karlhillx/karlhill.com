Oddbird is the Swedish producer that documents its dealcoholization process more openly than almost anyone in the category — vacuum distillation at low temperature, applied to conventionally vinified wine. The bundle SKU is a mixed case that doesn't itemize its contents in the order confirmation, which makes it a sampler rather than a specific purchase: you're buying the method and the producer, not a particular grape. The published review of Oddbird's Blanc de Blancs on this desk scored 84, and the range runs across sparkling and still wines with the same process behind all of them.

As a bundle entry, this is the honest version of what a mixed case should be: not a dumping ground for surplus stock, but a deliberate way to explore a producer whose whole range shares a sourcing discipline. The individual bottles are reviewed separately on this desk; this entry exists because the SKU was purchased as a bundle and should be tracked as one.
