Penn's Best NA is the value shelf of the non-alcoholic beer category — an American lager from the Fred Koch Brewery (also associated with Genesee Brewing), described as "naturally brewed" with water, malt, yeast and hops, at under 0.5% ABV and 70 calories a bottle. It's the beer that exists to be cheap, and it's honest about that.

The glass is what you'd expect from a budget American lager at zero proof: light malt, a faint grain note, a clean but unremarkable palate, and a finish that doesn't linger. There's no craft pretension here and no attempt to compete with the Athletic/Brew Dog tier. For someone who wants an NA beer in quantity — a case for the fridge, a fishing trip, a designated-driver situation — this is the option that doesn't require thinking about it. It does that job.
