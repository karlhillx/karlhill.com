Trabanco Sidra Sin Alcohol is dealcoholized Asturian sidra natural from Asturias, Spain — bitter, acidic, and acidulated apples fermented in chestnut, then stripped of alcohol. It is not juice dressed as cider and not a formulated alternative. The bottle in hand reads Sin Alcohol, solo 42 kcal, and under 0.5% vol on a classic green 70 cl sidra shape.

Poured from height, it wakes the way Asturian sidra should: sharp sour apple, clean acid, a little cider-house funk, and a light bead instead of soft-drink foam. The dealcoholization shows as lift and thinness rather than as sweet fake fruit. You miss some of the alcoholic culín’s weight, but you keep the ritual and the apple varieties’ bite.

Serve it cold and keep pouring high. That is the point of this bottle.
