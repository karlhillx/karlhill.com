Torres calls Serena Chile's first premium dealcoholized wine and then, on the technical sheet, gets specific: destemmed Sauvignon Blanc, 16 days at 12–16°C, "the wine then undergoes a dealcoholization process." Alcohol is printed 0.0% ABV; residual sugar 36 g/L. The machine is not named. That is still a sourced Yes — fermented wine, alcohol removed — with an honest method line.

The glass is citrus and grass with more sweetness than a dry Casablanca SB. Useful, documented, not a spinning-cone essay.
