DC Brau is a Washington brewery. This NA pale ale is on the Metro and Total Wine receipts in this house. The brewery homepage confirms the producer and city. It does not publish an NA process page, so ABV and method stay off the facts list.

The can is a pale ale, not a hop-water. Biscuit, light citrus, a short finish. A local bottle, not a national argument.
