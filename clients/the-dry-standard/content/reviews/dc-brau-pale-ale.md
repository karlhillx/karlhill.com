DC Brau is a Washington brewery. This NA pale ale is on the Metro and Total Wine receipts in this house. CEO Brandon Skall told DC Beer directly what took them so long: "In order to do it right we needed a separator to remove the alcohol from finished beer," a piece of equipment the brewery bought specifically to make this beer. Combined with a hybrid low-alcohol Lallemand yeast, the finished beer lands at 0.3% ABV — real beer, brewed to strength, then run through the separator.

The can is a pale ale, not a hop-water. Biscuit, light citrus, a short finish. A local bottle, not a national argument.
