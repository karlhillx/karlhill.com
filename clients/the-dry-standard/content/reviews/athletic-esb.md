Athletic's ESB is a limited-release pilot beer — online only, sold out at the time of this review — and it's the brewery taking on a style that few NA producers attempt. An Extra Special Bitter is all about balance: caramel malt sweetness against English hop bitterness, neither dominating, with a smooth body that makes it sessionable. Athletic's version, under their proprietary NA process, gets that balance right. The factory floor stays unpublished, but Bill Shufelt and John Walker have said on the record (Fast Company, Popular Mechanics) that they do not brew a full-strength beer and strip the alcohol, and that the method is not simple arrested fermentation either. That is enough to classify it as naturally low alcohol. The beers are built under 0.5% from the start.

The pour is caramel malt and gentle hops, smooth and clean on the palate, with the bitterness carrying the finish politely. As a style exercise it's excellent — more nuanced than the Run Wild IPA, more traditional, and clearly brewed by people who know what an ESB is supposed to taste like. If it returns from its sold-out status, it's worth seeking out for anyone who drinks English ales.
