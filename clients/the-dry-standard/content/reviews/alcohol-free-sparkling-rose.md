This entry is the exception of the batch: a house-label bottling that arrived on this desk through a grocery order with no brand identity attached, and which we've been unable to trace to a named producer. We're publishing it because the entry exists in the cellar record and because it represents a real and growing part of the category — supermarkets bottling anonymous alcohol-free sparkling under their own labels, sold cheaply, with no producer and no method on the label.

What can be said honestly is limited. The wine is a sparkling rosé, sold under a house label through US grocery retail, at a price point that puts it at the bottom of the category. In the glass it's soft and sweet-leaning with indistinct berry fruit — not offensive, not memorable, and not identifiable. If a named producer takes responsibility for this bottling, this entry should be replaced with their version; until then, this stands as an accurate record of what most supermarket NA sparkling actually is: anonymous, cheap, and adequate for the job.
