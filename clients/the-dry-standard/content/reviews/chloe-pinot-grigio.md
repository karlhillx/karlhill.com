Chloe Alcohol-Removed Pinot Grigio is dealcoholized California Pinot Grigio. Chloe’s page confirms alcohol removal without naming the machine; commercial product data puts the bottle at 0.5% ABV.

The nose is citrus and green apple — the Pinot Grigio aromatic signature, clean and light.

On the palate, crisp and easy-drinking; the fruit is simple and pleasant, the body lighter than a full-strength version.

The finish is clean and brief. The everyday Chloe white without the ethanol — light, citrusy, and priced for the shelf rather than the cellar.
