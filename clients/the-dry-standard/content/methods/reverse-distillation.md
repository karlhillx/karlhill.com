---
title: Reverse distillation
slug: reverse-distillation
summary: "A still run backward: high-proof flavored spirit goes in, ethanol comes off, a non-alcoholic concentrate stays in the pot."
order: 5
---

Spiritless uses this phrase in public. Kentucky 74 begins as high-proof neutral grain spirit and oak, not as a dump of aged bourbon. A first still extracts wood. A second still drives ethanol away and keeps the flavored remainder.

That is alcohol removal. It is also not the same as dealcoholizing a bottled whiskey. Reviews should say both sentences, in that order.

Expect thickeners and a little leftover ethanol. Brands that use reverse distillation often treat a trace of ABV as mouthfeel. Check the number they print this year — Spiritless's public figures have not always agreed with each other.
