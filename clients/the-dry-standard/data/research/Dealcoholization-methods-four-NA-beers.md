# Dealcoholization methods — four NA beers

**Accessed:** 2026-09-20  
**Products:** Erdinger Alkoholfrei · Flying Dog Deep Fake · Penn's Best NA Lager · Pure Project Grounded  
**Status:** Method **Unclassified** for all four. Production sites revised from COLA / press.  
**Caveat:** Much of the web pass used page summaries (WebFetch), not raw text; cans, Reddit, and some social captions were not read. “Not found” is a moderate-strength negative, not proof of non-disclosure.

## Bottom line

None of the four has a **verifiable method**. No producer statement, third-party report of one, equipment-supplier case study, or regulator record found says whether each beer is dealcoholized after fermentation or made by arrested / limited fermentation. Any named method for these SKUs is unsourced inference.

What the research **did** establish is where three are made (TTB COLA), and it overturned first-round home-brewery assumptions for Deep Fake and Grounded. A brewing site does **not** reveal a method, but it changes who to ask.

## Method search (negative)

Launch / product / press language for Deep Fake (“tweaked” each stage), Grounded (“crafted with the same care”), Penn’s Best (“naturally brewed and aged”), and Erdinger (Reinheitsgebot, residual &lt;0.5%, Freiburg residual-alcohol study) **fit either route** and are not method evidence.

**Do not cite as Erdinger’s method:** Stuttgarter Zeitung / VinePair industry-general vacuum distillation next to an Erdinger mention; BRAUWELT Rugenbräu membrane; Farny GEA RO.

**Weak inferences that are not method evidence:** Gärungskohlensäure on Erdinger ingredients; “naturally brewed”; Genesee N.A. “brewed like a beer” (different product); yeast on Grounded COLA; class-907 “near beer.”
