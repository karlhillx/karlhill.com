# Method disclosure gap — four NA beers (Sep 2026)

**Status:** research updated; vocabulary = Unclassified / Undeclared / Withheld.
**Scope:** Erdinger Alkoholfrei, Flying Dog Deep Fake NA IPA, Penn's Best NA Lager, Pure Project Grounded NA IPA.
**Editor note:** Do not contact breweries from this file alone — outreach is a separate editorial decision.

**Bottom line:** Exact technique remains undisclosed for all four. Public labels: production type **Unclassified** (`not-verified`); disclosure **Undeclared** by default, **Withheld** for Erdinger. Pure Project’s plant of record (Prost / Northglenn) is identified without disclosing the NA method.

## Tool reliability caveat

Tavily (and similar) auto-generated “answer” summaries confidently asserted brand-specific methods (e.g. Erdinger “uses vacuum distillation and reverse osmosis,” Pure Project “uses arrested fermentation”) that were **not** supported by the underlying snippets — those were generic industry explainers. Do not promote tool summaries to `provenance` or `production_type`. Record only what the cited page actually says about the named brand.

## Findings

### Erdinger Alkoholfrei (TDS-0066)

| Field | Finding |
| --- | --- |
| Dealcoholized vs brewed-low | Unclear — brand refuses to say |
| Method | Deliberately undisclosed (on-record) |
| Residual ABV | Intentional non-zero trace (&lt;0.5%) for flavor / vitamins |
| Confidence | High (secrecy policy) / None (named technique) |

- **On-record refusal:** [WELT, 2010](https://www.welt.de/wissenschaft/article10444678/Alkoholfreies-Bier-Bei-alkoholfreiem-Bier-ist-die-Herstellung-entscheidend.html) — Josef Westermeier (Managing Director, Marketing & Sales): recipe and brewing process are the foundation of success and will be kept private (“Dazu machen wir keine Angaben…”).
- **Producer FAQ PDF:** [Unbedenklicher, minimaler Alkoholgehalt](https://erdinger.de/downloads/Unbedenklicher_minimaler_Alkoholgehalt.pdf) — Erdinger brews Alkoholfrei so residual alcohol remains (&lt;0.5%) to keep full flavor and vitamins; argues full 0.0% paths that rely on unnamed “physical processes” strip aroma. That is **not** a named house method — do not upgrade to vacuum distillation / RO.
- Product pages remain silent on technique; industry-norm Weissbier NA process reviews that never name Erdinger stay unattributed.
