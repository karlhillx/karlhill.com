Dealcoholized: Yes

Method: Dealcoholized cider from cider apples, perry pears, and dealcoholized red wine, plus hops, herbs, and flowers

Product overview

Geiger works from meadow fruit in Schlat, not from a grape brief. Delmosa, the US importer, lists this rosé as dealcoholized cider from cider apples and perry pears plus dealcoholized red wine, hops, herbs, and flowers. Alcohol is under 0.5%. The can is 330 ml.

That is a sourced Yes for the cider and the red-wine cut. The specific still is not named on the product page. The glass does not need it: berry, hop bitterness, a sweet-sour argument most NA rosés never attempt.

Tasting notes

Nose

Red berries and a tart hop edge — more orchard than cellar.

Palate

Sweet-sour, bitter at the edges, the red-wine cut giving tannin the cider would not have alone.

Finish

Dry-tart, lingering hop. A food cider, not a picnic soda.

How to drink it

Cold, 45–50°F, in a tumbler.

Best for: Grill, a solo tart pour, anyone bored of NA wine's mid-palate.

Verdict

Not a wine trying to be cider or a cider trying to be wine. Apple, perry, a splash of dealcoholized red, hops. Tart in the way Geiger usually is.
