Christian Drouin Jus de Poire Pétillant

Cider · Normandy (Coudray-Rabut), France · Method unpublished

This is pressed pear juice with carbonation, not a fermented pear cider with the alcohol removed — the producer markets it as non-alcoholic pear juice.

Eric Bordelet

Eric Bordelet Perlant Jus de Pommes à Sydre

Cider · Normandy (Pays d'Auge), France · Method unpublished

Pressed apple juice from cider varieties with carbon dioxide added — never fermented, so there is no alcohol to remove. Distinct from Bordelet's alcoholic ciders.
86
