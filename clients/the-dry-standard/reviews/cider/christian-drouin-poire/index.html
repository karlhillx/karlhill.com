Christian Drouin Jus de Poire Pétillant

Christian Drouin · Cider

Christian Drouin's Jus de Poire Pétillant is pressed juice from Normandy heritage pears, lightly sparkling: never fermented, never stripped, just fruit.

Dealcoholized: No

Product facts

84/100

Is it actually dealcoholized?

This is pressed pear juice with carbonation, not a fermented pear cider with the alcohol removed — the producer markets it as non-alcoholic pear juice.

Base beverage: Pressed juice of local Normandy pear varieties (Plant de Blanc, Muscadet, Avenelle, Poire de Grise)

Product overview

Domaine Christian Drouin is a Normandy Calvados house — they make apple and pear brandy — and the Jus de Poire Pétillant is what happens when the same family that distills pears for a living presses juice instead. It’s not a fermented cider with the alcohol removed, and it isn’t pretending to be: it’s the juice of heritage Normandy pear varieties (Plant de Blanc, Muscadet, Avenelle, Poire de Grise), lightly carbonated, and nothing else. The absence of alcohol isn’t a feature engineered into it; it’s simply what pear juice is.

That makes it one of the very few drinks on this shelf that can’t be accused of compromise, because there’s no compromise happening. Ripe pear skin, a floral top note, gentle natural sweetness balanced by the fruit’s own acidity, and a clean short finish. At $15 a bottle it’s priced like a good juice rather than a wine, which is exactly right. For a cheese course, an afternoon, or anyone who’s tired of NA products that imitate something else — this is the real thing being itself.

Tasting notes

Nose

Ripe pear skin and a floral lift — fresh, clean, unmistakably real pear rather than pear flavoring.

Palate

Lightly sparkling and gently sweet; the pear's own acidity gives it structure instead of syrup.

Finish

Clean and short, the fruit fading naturally.

How to drink it

Well chilled, in a wine glass or a tumbler.

Best for: Anyone who wants the pear version of a good apple juice — and pairing with cheese, especially Norman ones.

Verdict

Not a dealcoholized anything — pressed Normandy pear juice from a Calvados house, lightly sparkling, and better for it. The purest expression of pear on this desk.
