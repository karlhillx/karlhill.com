ViSecco Pinot Meunier

Jörg Geiger's ViSecco Pinot Meunier is a vacuum-distilled organic sparkling wine refined with meadow fruits, herbs and blossoms: 0% alcohol, dry, and unlike anything else on the shelf.

Dealcoholized: Yes

85/100

Is it actually dealcoholized?

Dealcoholized: Yes

Method: Vacuum distillation of organic Sauvignon Blanc and Pinot Meunier base wines, then refined with meadow fruits, herbs and blossoms (producer)

Base beverage: Organic Pinot Meunier and Sauvignon Blanc wines, vinified conventionally before dealcoholization

Product overview

Jörg Geiger is a German producer who treats non-alcoholic wine as a creative medium rather than a subtraction exercise, and the ViSecco line is the clearest expression of that: organic Pinot Meunier and Sauvignon Blanc base wines, dealcoholized by vacuum distillation, then refined with infusions of meadow fruits, herbs and blossoms. The additions aren’t a cover-up for a thin wine; they’re the point — a second layer of flavour built on top of a wine that was made properly first.

The result is dry, elegant and genuinely unusual. Where most dealcoholized sparklings lean sweet to compensate for the missing weight, this one leans herbal: the Pinot Meunier fruit sits alongside grassy, floral notes from the infusion, and the finish carries both. It’s the sort of bottle that would be interesting even if alcohol were available everywhere, which is the highest compliment a zero-proof drink can earn. Around CHF 16 in Swiss retail and £12.80 for the half-bottle in the UK, it’s priced like a serious aperitif — and it drinks like one.

Tasting notes

Nose

Pinot Meunier fruit and herbal notes from the botanical infusion — floral, slightly grassy, distinctive.

Palate

Dry and elegant; the wine base gives it structure while the meadow-fruit infusion adds a herbal complexity that no straight dealcoholized sparkling has.

Finish

Dry and long, the herbs and blossom notes lingering.

How to drink it

Well chilled, in a flute or wine glass.

Best for: Anyone tired of NA sparkling that tastes like sweet apple juice — this reads as a serious aperitif.

Verdict

Not content to just strip alcohol — Jörg Geiger dealcoholizes organic Pinot Meunier by vacuum distillation, then adds back meadow fruits, herbs and blossoms. The result is a genuinely original drink.
