Is it actually dealcoholized?

Dealcoholized: Yes

Method: 75% dealcoholized red wine blended with damson plum, currant, and blackberry juice plus herb and wildflower extracts

Product overview

The user of this cellar already knows Geiger made this. Delmosa prints the recipe: 75% dealcoholized red wine, damson plum, currant, blackberry, herb and wildflower extracts, sulfites, still, 750 ml, under 0.5% ABV. The grape variety of that red is not on the product page, so it is not invented here.

This is not a 100% wine and does not need to be. The dealcoholized fraction is the badge. The rest is why it has tannin and spice when most NA reds have only juice.

Tasting notes

Nose

Cassis, plum, a little lavender and spice — darker than the cider rosé.

Palate

Grip and acidity, dark cherry, cocoa, pepper. The tannin is the reason to open it with food.

Finish

Spice and dry fruit. Not a sweet red.

How to drink it

Cool, 55–60°F. Not cellar-cold.

Best for: Braise, grill, anything that wants a still red without ethanol weight.

Verdict

A dealcoholized red that admits it is also fruit and herbs. Cassis, plum, tannin, and a structure most NA reds fake with juice alone.
