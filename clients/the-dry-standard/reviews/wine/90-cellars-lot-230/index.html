90+ Cellars Lot 230 Non-Alcoholic Sparkling Brut

90+ Cellars Lot 230 is a South West French dealcoholized sparkling brut from Souvignier Gris: under 0.5% ABV, 25 calories, around $13 a bottle.

Dealcoholized: Not verified

Is it actually dealcoholized?

Dealcoholized: Not verified

Base beverage: Souvignier Gris (a German-bred Piwi hybrid grape resistant to downy mildew)

Product overview

The grape alone makes Lot 230 interesting: Souvignier Gris, a modern Piwi hybrid bred in Germany for disease resistance and increasingly planted across Europe for organic-friendly viticulture. 90+ Cellars sources it from South West France and dealcoholizes the finished brut, which gives them a sparkling NA that’s genuinely cheap to produce at the source — the $12.99 Binny’s shelf price reflects that. It is the lowest priced dealcoholized sparkling on a US shelf that isn’t a relabeled bulk gallon, and for $13, that’s a credential worth filing.

The drink itself is what you’d expect from a clean-brut Souvignier Gris: green-apple and lemon, a faint bready note from the secondary fermentation, a crisp, brief finish. Compared to a Mionetto or a French Bloom it lacks the varietal fingerprint of a real Prosecco or Chardonnay — Souvignier Gris is a workhorse grape, not a showpiece — but it does its job in a flute, with a glass of something beside it (oyster, fritto misto, an afternoon on a porch). The cheapest credible pour in the NA sparkling category, and the friendliest entry point for anyone who wants to know if this whole aisle is for them.

Tasting notes

Nose

Light and bready, with a green-apple note and the faint citrus that Souvignier Gris tends toward.

Palate

Bright and dry; green-apple and lemon with a crisp finish, lower body than a dealcoholized traditional brut but well-cut for the format.

Finish

Clean and brief, citrus-forward.

How to drink it

Chilled in a flute.

Best for: Bottom-shelf NA spritzes — the cheapest credible pour in the dealcoholized sparkling category.

Verdict

The most interesting NA sparkling of the low-cost tier — a Souvignier Gris from South West France at $12.99, which makes it the cheapest dealcoholized sparkling wine on this shelf.
