Sources disagree on the following points. We do not pick a winner.

  • Origin: Total Wine's highlight copy says Germany; the brand is Wölffer Estate of New York. We treat the wine as German-made under a New York estate label and do not collapse the two.

Product overview

Wölffer is a Sagaponack estate. Total Wine’s page for this bottle says Germany in the tasting copy, New York in the brand field, 0.0% ABV, organically grown, alcohol-removed, $20.99. That contradiction is recorded. The method is not named, so the badge is not-verified.

The glass is the best cheap-adjacent sparkling rosé on this list: berry, peach, acidity. Pay the extra ten dollars over Rondel if you want mouthfeel.

Tasting notes

Nose

Red berry, ripe peach, fresh pear.

Palate

Vibrant acidity, elegant fruit, a finer mouthfeel than the $10 NA pinks.

Finish

Clean, a little earthy. More wine than soda.

How to drink it

Well chilled.

Best for: The nicer toast on this list.

Verdict

A Long Island estate name on a German-made alcohol-removed sparkling rosé. Red berry, peach, real acidity. Method still a label phrase.
