Blanc de Blanc Non-Alcoholic Sparkling Wine (Jörg Geiger)

Jörg Geiger's Blanc de Blanc is organic Chardonnay and Colombard, bottle-fermented and aged two years on the lees before vacuum distillation: brioche, apple, gooseberry and blossom.

Dealcoholized: Yes

87/100

Is it actually dealcoholized?

Dealcoholized: Yes

Method: Organic Chardonnay and Colombard wine, bottle-fermented and matured on the lees for two years, then vacuum distilled (producer)

Base beverage: Organic Chardonnay and Colombard, conventionally vinified with secondary bottle fermentation and lees ageing before dealcoholization

Product overview

This is the most technically serious non-alcoholic sparkling on the desk, and the method explains why: organic Chardonnay and Colombard are bottle-fermented and then matured on the lees for two years — the same process that gives Champagne its brioche character — before the alcohol is removed by vacuum distillation. Lees ageing before dealcoholization is unusual because the process is expensive and the payoff invisible to anyone reading a label; the payoff, though, is in the glass.

It shows up as a mousse with genuine structure and a nose of brioche and baked apple that no shortcut method produces. The palate brings gooseberry, tangerine and orchard fruit over more mid-palate weight than dealcoholized sparklings normally manage, and the finish is toasty and long. At $34 in US retail and £32 in the UK it’s priced in the upper tier of the category, and it earns the position: this is the bottle to open for someone who thinks non-alcoholic sparkling is a compromise.

Tasting notes

Nose

Brioche and baked apple with a floral lift — the lees ageing leaves a genuine Champagne-adjacent aromatic signature.

Palate

Structured and dry; orchard fruit, gooseberry and tangerine over a fine mousse, with more mid-palate weight than most dealcoholized sparklings achieve.

Finish

Long and toasty, the brioche note extending the finish.

How to drink it

Chilled, in a flute or white-wine glass.

Best for: The dinner-party bottle, and the answer to anyone who says NA sparkling can't be serious.

Verdict

Two years on the lees before vacuum distillation — that's how you keep brioche character in a zero-proof sparkling. The most serious NA sparkling on this desk.
