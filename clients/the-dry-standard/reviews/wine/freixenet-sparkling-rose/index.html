Freixenet 0,0 Sparkling Rosé

Freixenet's alcohol-removed sparkling rosé is Airén-led, under 0.05% ABV, and 38 g/L residual sugar. The removal method is still unpublished.

Base beverage: Blend of classic Spanish grapes, Airén dominant

Product overview

This is not the 0,0 Sparkling White already reviewed. It is the rosé SKU, bought at Metro, listed by Freixenet as alcohol-removed. The US and Spanish pages give ABV under 0.05%, Airén-dominant fruit, and 38 g/L residual sugar from 2022. They do not name vacuum distillation, reverse osmosis, or a cone. The badge stays not-verified, same house rule as the white.

The glass is sweeter pink fizz: berry, soft bead, a short finish. Useful. Not a method lesson.

Tasting notes

Nose

Red berry and a little citrus. Fruit-forward, not yeasty.

Palate

Soft bead, noticeable sweetness, easy red fruit. The sugar does more work than the acidity.

Finish

Short and candy-adjacent if it warms. Keep it iced.

How to drink it

6–8°C, flute.

Best for: A cheap toast, salad, the bottle you do not overthink.

Verdict

The pink twin of the 0,0 Sparkling White already here. Same unpublished method, same 38 g/L sugar, more berry. Competent supermarket fizz.
