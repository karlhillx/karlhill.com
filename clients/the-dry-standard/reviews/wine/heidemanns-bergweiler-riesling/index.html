Dealcoholized: Not verified

Product overview

The estate is real and Mosel. The NA Riesling is on the Instacart receipts. The estate site confirms producer and place, not the dealcoholization step, so the badge is not-verified and ABV is omitted.

The glass is a light, off-dry Riesling. Not Eins-Zwei-Zero. A Mosel-shaped supermarket white.

Tasting notes

Nose

Lime and green apple, a faint slate note.

Palate

Off-dry, light, Mosel-shaped acidity without ethanol weight.

Finish

Short citrus. The sugar shows as it warms.

How to drink it

Well chilled.

Best for: Weeknight fish when you want a German Riesling label.

Verdict

A Mosel name on an NA Riesling. Citrus and a little residual sweetness, thinner than Leitz, better documented origin than most Total Wine whites.
