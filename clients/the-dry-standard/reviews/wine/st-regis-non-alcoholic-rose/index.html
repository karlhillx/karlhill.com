Product overview

St. Regis is not affiliated with the St. Regis hotel chain — a question worth settling upfront, since the name invites the assumption. This is a standalone dealcoholized-wine brand, made in Germany, sold in the US primarily through Total Wine, where this Syrah/Shiraz rosé (SKU 81590750) has sat in the non-alcoholic aisle at under $13 a bottle.

The producer is direct about method: full fermentation and aging as a conventional wine, followed by vacuum distillation to remove the alcohol while preserving aroma and color. That earns it a “yes” on dealcoholized, distinct from formulated zero-proof rosés that never contained alcohol to begin with.

Where sources diverge is taste. Total Wine’s own product description is mild and pleasant — light pink, smooth, strawberry and raspberry, soft finish. A third-party reviewer who bought the same SKU in 2023 described a sour, almost kombucha-like first pour that mellowed somewhat by the second night. We’re not picking a winner between a retailer’s marketing copy and one blogger’s palate; both are recorded, and readers should treat the “soft, fruity” description as the seller’s framing rather than a consensus.

Tasting notes

Strawberry and raspberry aromas (retailer description).

Palate

Light pink and smooth; retailer notes describe strawberry and raspberry carrying through to the palate with a soft finish.

Finish

Soft, per retailer description.

How to drink it

Chilled, in a white-wine or rosé glass.

Best for: Mild cheeses, crab cakes, or other simple pairings, per the retailer's own suggestion.

Verdict

A budget-friendly, widely stocked dealcoholized rosé with a legitimate removal method behind it, though reviewer accounts diverge sharply on how well it drinks.
