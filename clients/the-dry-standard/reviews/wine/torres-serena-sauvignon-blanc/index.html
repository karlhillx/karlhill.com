Torres Serena Sauvignon Blanc

Serena Mode is Central Valley Sauvignon Blanc, fermented then dealcoholized to 0.0% ABV per the 2024 technical sheet.

Dealcoholized: Yes

81/100

Is it actually dealcoholized?

Dealcoholized: Yes

Method: Fermented 16 days, then the finished wine undergoes a dealcoholization process (technique not named)

Base beverage: 100% Sauvignon Blanc, destemmed, fermented 16 days at 12–16°C

Product overview

Torres calls Serena Chile’s first premium dealcoholized wine and then, on the technical sheet, gets specific: destemmed Sauvignon Blanc, 16 days at 12–16°C, “the wine then undergoes a dealcoholization process.” Alcohol is printed 0.0% ABV; residual sugar 36 g/L. The machine is not named. That is still a sourced Yes — fermented wine, alcohol removed — with an honest method line.

The glass is citrus and grass with more sweetness than a dry Casablanca SB. Useful, documented, not a spinning-cone essay.

Tasting notes

Nose

Citrus and a grassy edge, quieter than a 13% Casablanca bottle.

Palate

36 g/L sugar makes it plumper than the nose suggests. Lime and green fruit over a light body.

Finish

Short citrus. The residual sugar lingers more than the herb.

How to drink it

8–10°C.

Best for: Shellfish and grilled fish, as Torres suggests, or a weekday white.

Verdict

A Chilean Sauvignon Blanc that was wine first. Torres prints 0.0% and 'dealcoholization process' without naming the machine. The grape still shows.
