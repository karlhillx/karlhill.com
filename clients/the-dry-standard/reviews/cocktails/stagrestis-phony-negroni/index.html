St. Agrestis Phony Negroni

St. Agrestis · Cocktails

St. Agrestis' Phony Negroni is the brand's flagship NA cocktail: 30 organic, non-GMO botanicals, 0.0% ABV, and the closest any formulated NA negroni gets to the bitter-piney Italian original.

Dealcoholized: No

Product facts

86/100

Is it actually dealcoholized?

St. Agrestis cocktails are formulated from a base of 30 organic, non-GMO botanicals — not dealcoholized from a once-alcoholic Negroni.

Base beverage: 30 organic, non-GMO botanicals designed to replicate negroni aromatics without any underlying spirit.

Product overview

St. Agrestis doesn’t call itself a cocktail company — it calls itself “drinks for recovering hedonists” out of Greenpoint, and the Phony Negroni is the flagship. The conceit is that a Negroni isn’t really about alcohol; it’s about the way bitter orange, gentian and juniper build a structure around the gin. So they build the same structure from a base of 30 organic, non-GMO botanicals, no alcohol ever present, no real Campari ever crossed, and a 0.0% ABV number that doesn’t need to apologize for itself.

The pour reaches the bar. Piney bitterness up top, gentian on the mid-palate, an orange-citrus lift that reads unmistakably as Negroni-adjacent without being a copy of the Italian original, and a bone-dry finish closer to a Cocchi Americano rinse than to a juice mocktail. Build it like a Negroni — over a large ice cube, orange peel — and the ritual works. VinePair named it the 2025 Rising Brand of the Year for a reason: at a category this young, with the consumer search for “what can I drink at 6 PM” running hotter every quarter, this is currently the best answer.

Tasting notes

Nose

Piney and bitter, gentian and wormwood, layered with a citrusy high note that's unmistakably Negroni-adjacent without borrowing.

Palate

Bitter and botanical, the gentian carrying the middle, the citrus and herbal notes framing it; bone-dry, no sugar overlay.

Finish

Long and bitter — closer to a Cocchi Americano rinse than to a juice mocktail.

How to drink it

Build like a real Negroni — pour over a large ice cube, a curl of orange peel. The canned format needs less help than expected.

Best for: The 6 PM hour when nothing else on the NA shelf gets close to an Italian aperitivo.

Verdict

VinePair's 2025 Rising Drinks Brand of the Year is, by a sensible margin, the closest NA pour to an actual Negroni. Built from scratch with 30 botanicals — and built right.
