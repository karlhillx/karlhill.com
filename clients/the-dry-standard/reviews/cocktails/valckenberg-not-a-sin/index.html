Product overview

Valckenberg, the same German house behind the Zero Riesling and Sparkling, makes a small detour into the aperitivo category with ‘It’s Not a Sin’ — a formulated NA drink built around ginger and bitter orange. There’s no wine base here; it’s a botanical formulation designed to be spritzed, the way you’d build an Aperol Spritz but without the Aperol.

The bitter orange leads with the ginger providing the warmth that gentian or quinine would in the Italian aperitivo family. It’s a competent entry in a category that’s growing quickly — the NA spritz shelf — and the Valckenberg name brings the wine-world credibility that most aperitivo brands lack. Priced accessibly and served over ice with sparkling water, it does the job of the late-afternoon aperitivo honestly.

Tasting notes

Nose

Bright bitter orange with a ginger warmth underneath.

Palate

Bittersweet and gently spiced; the ginger does the work that gentian does in an Aperol, the orange carries the fruit side.

Finish

Clean and gently bitter.

How to drink it

Over ice with sparkling water and an orange slice.

Best for: The aperitivo hour, spritzed over ice with sparkling water.

Verdict

Valckenberg's contribution to the NA aperitivo shelf — ginger and bitter orange, built as a spritz base rather than a wine substitute.
