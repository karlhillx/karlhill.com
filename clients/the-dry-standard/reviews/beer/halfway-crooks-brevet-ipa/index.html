Product overview

The Brevet Pils already has a review. This is the IPA, purchased more often in this house. Untappd files it as a non-alcoholic IPA from Halfway Crooks. The brewery site still leads with the pilsner. No ABV and no method are cited here because they are not on a page we can stand behind.

The can drinks like an IPA that was never asked to be 6%. Citrus, a little pine, a clean finish. Same Atlanta discipline as the pils, less documentation.

Tasting notes

Nose

Citrus peel and a crackery malt line — hoppier than the pils, still tidy.

Palate

Light IPA bitterness, lemon and a little pine, body closer to a session beer than a 7% West Coast.

Finish

Dry and short. No worty leftover.

How to drink it

Very cold.

Best for: The Metro run when the pils is gone.

Verdict

The hoppier Brevet. Same Atlanta can, less public science than the pilsner already on this desk. Drink it as an IPA that happens to be NA.
